      How it works

      1. Gather

        Every 5 minutes the terminal collects prices, SEC insider filings and Congress disclosures from public sources, and keeps the history.

      2. Connect

        It links each stock to the insiders and politicians behind it and tallies a signal score.

      3. Explain

        Caesar turns it into a briefing you can read or hear, then answers your questions by voice.
