      Insiders, the day they file

      Company directors and big shareholders must report their own trades within two business days. The terminal checks the SEC every 5 minutes on weekdays and keeps the open-market buys and sales.

      What Congress is buying

      Members of Congress have to disclose their trades, often weeks later. We gather the filings, spot when several of them buy the same stock, and tell you how late the news is.

      1. Gather

      2. Connect

        It links each stock to the insiders and politicians behind it and tallies a signal score.

      3. Explain
